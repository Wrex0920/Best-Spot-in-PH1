Boracay

★★★★★ Popular Beach Destination
Beach Island Relaxation

Boracay is a popular island destination in the Philippines known for White Beach, beautiful sunsets, and clear turquoise waters. Visitors can enjoy swimming, island hopping, and other beach activities.

The island also offers many hotels, restaurants, and activities, making it a great place for travelers looking for a relaxing beach vacation.

Puerto Princesa Underground River

★★★★★ Natural Wonder
Nature Adventure Palawan

Located in Palawan, the Puerto Princesa Subterranean River is known for its underground river and impressive limestone cave formations. It is one of the country's most famous natural attractions.

Chocolate Hills

★★★★★ Bohol Landmark
Nature Sightseeing Bohol

The Chocolate Hills in Bohol consist of many cone-shaped hills spread across the landscape. During the dry season, the grass covering the hills turns brown, giving them their chocolate-like appearance.

Mayon Volcano

★★★★★ Scenic Mountain Destination
Mountain Nature Albay

Mayon Volcano is located in Albay and is well known for its symmetrical shape. Its impressive appearance makes it one of the most recognizable natural landmarks in the Philippines.

Siargao

★★★★★ Surfing Paradise
Surfing Beach Island

Siargao is a popular island destination famous for surfing, especially at Cloud 9. It also offers beautiful lagoons, beaches, and island-hopping experiences for visitors.
